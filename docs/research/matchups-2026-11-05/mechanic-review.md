## Selected pair-level findings

**Jax versus Garen and Aatrox:** the rule texts accurately limit the dodge to an attack. The current and historical outcome evidence does not justify promoting either as a strong whole-matchup recommendation. Garen's spin/ultimate and Aatrox's repeated spell damage remain. This is a recommendation-eligibility failure, not evidence that Counter Strike cannot dodge an eligible attack.

**Teemo, Nasus and Malphite versus Olaf:** the six-pick expansion references Tough It Out but omits Ragnarok from the referenced opponent abilities and meaningful failure conditions. The source says Olaf becomes immune to disables. The proposed blind/slow/cripple window needs explicit R downtime, and any recommendation must account for him choosing to activate R. These three rules are flagged `missing-defining-opponent-exception` in the audit.

**Nautilus versus Yuumi:** the statistical association is favourable, but the existing R explanation only applies when Yuumi is detached and targetable. Ordinary play must also consider engaging the host, the ADC pairing, level breakpoints, wave control and whether Yuumi can safely exit. A favourable game sample does not prove the narrow authored R plan is the reason for the advantage. This older authored rule is displayed in slot six, illustrating why slot number is not source-file origin.

**Ezreal versus Yasuo:** short repositioning can avoid a particular trajectory while the whole matchup remains unfavourable in the collected game samples. The entry omits much of the lane economy and how Yasuo interacts with Ezreal's wider kit. Avoiding one telegraphed spell is not sufficient pick evidence.

**Mel plans:** Riot's [26.22 notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-22-notes/) confirm projectile destruction/retaliation and shielding in the reworked W. The stored new description matches that. Previous-patch Mel samples still describe a materially different defensive tool, so a two-patch numeric match is not a valid unchanged-mechanic validation.

**Projectile and spell-shield families:** the candidate's own short description is insufficient to establish every opponent projectile type, shield-breaking order or delayed secondary effect. The community [spell-shield documentation](https://wiki.leagueoflegends.com/en-us/Spell_shield) records spell-specific and recurring-effect exceptions. It is supporting mechanic documentation, not ranked matchup outcome evidence. Generic references cannot replace checking each exact exception.

## What remains unverified

All entries retain `hardCounterCertified:false`. Pair-specific collision tests, lane metrics, rank-by-rank consistency, mastery effects, independent tactical review and best-rune outcome comparisons remain unverified. This file records inspected base facts and specific review failures without inventing a universal pass. These findings support separating matchup recommendations from conditional ability tools and removing a forced minimum count.
